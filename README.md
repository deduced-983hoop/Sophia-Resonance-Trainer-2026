## 🎮 About the Game

Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy is a third-person action-adventure game developed by Asobo Studio and released on August 27, 2026. The prequel follows Sophia through a story of combat, mythology, exploration, stealth, and environmental puzzles across medieval and ancient Minoan settings.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy**. This trainer enhances the single-player experience with survival, combat, stealth, and progression options designed around Sophia's melee-focused gameplay, parrying, dodging, finishing moves, exploration, and skill development.

**Target Audience:** Players who want a more relaxed story experience, easier combat, faster skill progression, or additional freedom while exploring Sophia's journey.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## ⚡ Features (v1.0)

### 🎯 Gameplay

- **God Mode** — Prevents Sophia from taking damage during exploration and combat.
- **Infinite Health** — Keeps Sophia's health fully restored while enabled.
- **Unlimited Skill Points** — Provides a large supply of progression points for unlocking available combat and exploration skills.
- **Stealth Mode** — Reduces enemy awareness while Sophia moves through stealth sections.

### 🧰 Inventory

- **Infinite Resources** — Prevents collected gameplay resources from being consumed during crafting and upgrades.

### ⚔️ Combat

- **Perfect Parry Window** — Extends the timing window for successful parries against incoming attacks.
- **Instant Finisher** — Allows vulnerable enemies to be finished immediately when the finisher state is available.
- **One-Hit Takedowns** — Greatly increases melee damage against standard enemies.
- **Freeze Enemies** — Freezes nearby enemies in place, making combat encounters easier to control.

### 🚶 Movement

- **No Fall Damage** — Protects Sophia from damage caused by falls during traversal and exploration.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Use the movement options during exploration, climbing, jumping, and grapple-based traversal. No Fall Damage is useful when testing shortcuts or navigating vertical environments, while Stealth Mode helps preserve a careful approach through guarded areas.

### Tools & Inventory

Infinite Resources supports crafting and progression by preventing collected materials from being depleted. Enable it before opening crafting or upgrade menus and disable it when you want the normal resource economy.

### World & Environment

Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy combines environmental puzzles, exploration, stealth encounters, and traversal across medieval and Minoan locations. God Mode and No Fall Damage are best used for exploration, while Stealth Mode helps compatibility unwanted encounters.

### Combat & Weapons

Sophia's combat is built around sword attacks, blocking, parrying, dodging, kicks, counters, and finishing moves. Perfect Parry Window makes defensive timing more forgiving, while Instant Finisher, One-Hit Takedowns, Freeze Enemies, and God Mode simplify difficult encounters.
